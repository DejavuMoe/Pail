Privacy and safety

No telemetry. No update check. No secrets in config files.

  • Keychain or encrypted vault

    Secret keys go to Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or Secret Service, or to an optional vault encrypted with Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305.

  • Only your endpoints

    Pail connects only to the endpoints of the accounts you add.

  • Logs off by default

    When you turn the diagnostic log on, signatures, authorization headers, query strings and known secrets are removed.

  • Scope before you confirm

    Destructive actions show what they will touch first. Deleting a bucket requires typing its name.
